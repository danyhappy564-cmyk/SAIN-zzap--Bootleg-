## SAIN Remix7.4

**- Cleaner settings + ZZAP and per-map settings per bot type (bot behaviour and zzap values same as Remix7.3; updating alone changes nothing)**

■ What's new
- New tab "ZZAP (tactics etc.)": 14 tactics such as diamond step and QEQE lean shooting as plain on/off switches; simulation settings get their own "Simulation" tab
- Bot settings in 5 tabs: Quick · Personality (pick it right there) · ZZAP · Per map · Advanced (one group at a time)
- ZZAP per bot type: Global / On / Off (e.g. no QEQE for PMCs only, scavs that diamond-step)
- Per-map multipliers per bot type: hearing, vision, spotting, aggression and more (e.g. lower hearing on Labs only, Reserve unchanged)
- English UI shows the preset names in English (file names unchanged, so your pick and [Modified] copies carry on)
- Compare with: Easy / Normal / zzap (Hard) / Saved; rename your own presets (F6 and web)

■ Install
- Extract SAIN.REMIX.7.4.7z into your SPT folder and overwrite (client and server both changed, settings and slots kept)
- On a clean install, back up your [Modified] presets and ClientData/botSettingsSlots.json first

■ Notes
- Per-map multipliers apply from the next raid
- Undo does not lower the "unsaved changes" count (since an earlier release; saving clears it)
